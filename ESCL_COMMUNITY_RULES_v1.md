# Elite Stock Car League — Community Rules v1.0

ESCL should feel competitive without becoming hostile.

Treat other players with respect. No harassment, threats, hate-based abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, targeted bullying, spam, scams, or sharing another person's private information.

Do not exploit bugs, automate gameplay unfairly, manipulate accounts, coordinate race outcomes dishonestly, or attempt to bypass commissioner/security controls. Report serious exploits instead of using them for an advantage.

Team rivalries, race-day trash talk, and competitive banter are welcome when everyone can still enjoy the league. Moderation may issue warnings, suspensions, or restore accounts when appropriate.

Closed-alpha rules may be refined as real community needs become clearer.
