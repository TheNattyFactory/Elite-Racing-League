# Elite Stock Car League — Terms of Use (Closed Alpha Draft v1.0)

ESCL is an experimental simulation service. Closed-alpha access may change, reset, pause, or end while the game is being developed.

Users must provide accurate account information, keep account credentials private, and use the service lawfully. Accounts may not be sold, shared for abuse, automated to gain unfair advantages, or used to disrupt races, league systems, or other users.

ESCL progression is intended to be earned through play. Exploits, unauthorized automation, manipulation of league systems, harassment, impersonation, spam, or attempts to bypass account/security controls may result in warnings or suspension.

Game rules, XP values, schedules, ratings, teams, and features may change during testing. Alpha statistics and careers may be reset when necessary for testing or launch preparation.

This draft is for closed-alpha product operations and should receive appropriate legal review before a broad public/commercial launch.
