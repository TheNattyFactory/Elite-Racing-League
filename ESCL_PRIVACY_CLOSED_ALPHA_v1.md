# Elite Stock Car League — Privacy Notice (Closed Alpha Draft v1.0)

ESCL stores information needed to operate accounts and careers, such as username, email address, authentication/session records, driver career data, league activity, and security/audit events.

Passwords are not stored in plaintext. Authentication uses salted password hashes. Verification and password-reset tokens are stored as hashes and expire.

Operational logs may be used to protect the service, investigate abuse, troubleshoot failures, and administer the league. ESCL should collect only information needed for the service and should not expose private account information to other players.

Closed-alpha users should be given a way to request account assistance or deletion before broader launch. Production hosting, retention periods, third-party email processing, and applicable privacy rights must be documented before public beta.

This is a closed-alpha draft and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.
